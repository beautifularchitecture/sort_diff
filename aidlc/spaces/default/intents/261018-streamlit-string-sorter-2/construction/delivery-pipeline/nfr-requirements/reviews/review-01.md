### Summary

The pipeline's two core behaviors — failing the run on deploy error and enforcing the 80% coverage floor — are asserted by the artifacts but unimplementable under the confirmed answers and Contract 2 as written (no deploy step exists under Q3 B, and bare `pytest` measures no coverage), and the push-to-main-only trigger silently breaks the merge-blocking claims the artifacts still make. The unit needs one reconciled deployment model, an enforceable coverage-gate mechanism, corrected derived NFR numbering, and the recorded answers before a developer can build from it without guessing.
