### Summary

The design is coherent and its security decisions (typed input, no evaluation, no markup, no I/O) trace to the requirements. It is not implementable as written, for two reasons. The shared-counter description would merge the three algorithms' counts into one total, which breaks FR4.2. traceability.json fails its sensor and does not enumerate the NFR sub-IDs the stage consumes. The caller obligations (output escaping and the 100-string cap) also need to appear in the shared contract.
